Amaury led partnerships for Fetch.ai and SingularityNET within the Artificial Superintelligence Alliance, one of the largest AI ecosystems in Web3. He then ran growth for the alliance's venture hub, with the goal of growing the revenues of its portfolio companies.

He built a business development team that sold on the companies' behalf and closed each deal end to end: finding the counterparty, working out what it needed and getting the agreement signed.

For the past six months he has brought the same approach to the industry-loss market, working alongside reinsurers, ILS funds, brokers, index providers and regulated US venues. He knows first-hand how each of these counterparties operates and what it takes to close with them.

His edge at Gami Capital is commercial execution over advisory.
