Léo has been active in DeFi since 2019 and has made more than 20 investments. At Stake Capital Group, which managed more than $250M, he was VC Associate and Asset Manager. He led pre-seed and seed investments across DeFi, infrastructure and tooling, with tickets from $100K to $1M.

He invested and followed on in Zama and Morpho, and executed secondary sales on Morpho. He also took part in two strategic acquisitions, of Backed Finance (issuer of the xStocks tokenised equities) by Kraken and of Kakarot, whose round he led, by Zama.

Before that, he taught DeFi at Alyra, France's leading blockchain school, and was Head of Blockchain at a Web3 development studio.

His edge is the combination of active risk management, venture-grade due diligence, live vault curation and product execution. Having worked as both a venture investor and an asset manager, Léo looks at a protocol as an investment to underwrite and as a position to hold through periods of stress and later exit, backed by a network of protocols, foundations and crypto VCs built since 2019.
