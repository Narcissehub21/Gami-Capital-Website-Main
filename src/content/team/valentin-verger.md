Valentin has been active in DeFi since 2019 and holds degrees in engineering and finance.

He spent three and a half years as Asset Manager at BlackPool DAO, managing close to $50M, and now leads its wind-down. He was then VC Associate at Modica Ventures, the former family office of CoinAcademy, the largest French-speaking crypto media, where he worked on about ten investments including Morpho, Peaq and GRVT. He is also active in deal sourcing and family office advisory.

At Gami Capital he structured and led the first twelve positions, among them [Turtle Club](/portfolio/turtle-club), [Lagoon](/portfolio/lagoon), [The Interfold](/portfolio/the-interfold) and [Concrete](/portfolio/concrete).

His edge is the combination of venture investing with hands-on experience in deal sourcing, SPV structuring and LP relations. He knows how liquidity moves, how incentives fade once emissions stop, how vaults behave under stress and what a listing does to a token's float, and he approaches these questions with the rigour of traditional finance. His network of founders, funds and market makers helps secure competitive allocations at entry and counterparties at exit.

He holds an engineering degree from UniLaSalle and a degree in economics and management, banking and finance track, from Université de Rennes 1.
