David founded Gami Capital SA in Geneva in 2022 to bring institutional discipline to onchain asset management. He also founded [Gami Labs](https://gamilabs.io/), the onchain asset management platform that works alongside Gami Capital, with more than $80M in assets under management. He has raised one fund.

He has been active in digital markets since 2015 and has more than eight years of experience trading financial markets, across equities, indices, commodities and precious metals. His background is in brokerage and principal trading.

This shapes how Gami handles risk: positions are sized and managed by someone who has carried them himself. David brings institutional-grade risk management and structuring to the firm, with direct access to allocators, family offices and tier-one counterparties built over a career on the principal side of the market.
