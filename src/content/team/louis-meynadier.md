Louis co-founded Good Partners, a syndicate of 70 LPs that has deployed $2M in DeFi and raised more than €2M for projects including Curvance and Silhouette. He previously worked in buy-side equity at BNP Paribas Wealth & Asset Management in Brussels, covering tech and financials, and before that at Opportunity Network, a deal-matching platform used by more than 52,000 CEOs and investors.

He has more than four years of DeFi venture experience and has analysed more than 2,000 projects. He has invested alongside Polychain and Pantera, in Silhouette, Curvance and others.

At Gami Capital, Louis covers onchain private credit, asset-based finance, and fixed-rate and lending markets. His underwriting looks at collateral, behaviour under stress, counterparties and how rates form. He works on positions such as [Pareto](/portfolio/pareto), [Tenka](/portfolio/tenka) and [SuperNova](/portfolio/supernova), the team behind Rates Exchange.

He works in French, English and Spanish.
