This policy explains what personal data Gami Capital SA collects through this website, why, and which rights you have. It is written for the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, for visitors in the European Economic Area, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

## Who is responsible

Gami Capital SA, Place de Longemalle 1, c/o MN & Associés SA, 1204 Geneva, Switzerland. For any question about your data, write to [contact@gami.ventures](mailto:contact@gami.ventures).

## When you browse the site

The site is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel's infrastructure processes technical data for each request, such as your IP address, the date and time, the page requested and your browser type, to deliver the site and protect it against abuse. These logs are kept for a short period and we do not use them to identify visitors.

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts are served from our own domain, and the Ethereum block number shown on the home page is fetched by our server. Your browser therefore does not contact any third party while you read the site. Third-party sites, such as LinkedIn, X or the investor portal, are only contacted if you click a link to them.

## When you write to us

If you use the contact form, we receive your role, name, email address, company, website or deck link, and message. The form is delivered to our mailbox through Resend, an email delivery service. If you write to us by email, we receive the content of your email.

We use this information only to answer you and to follow up on the conversation you started.

## Legal basis

Under the GDPR, we process contact data on the basis of your consent and of our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR). We process technical data on the basis of our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## How long we keep it

Messages are kept for as long as we need them to deal with your request and any relationship that follows from it. They are then deleted, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

## Who receives it

Our team, our hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) and our email delivery provider (Resend, Inc.), which process data on our behalf. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for marketing.

Vercel and Resend are based in the United States. Transfers rely on an adequacy decision, such as the Swiss-U.S. and EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on standard contractual clauses.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to [contact@gami.ventures](mailto:contact@gami.ventures).

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC) in Switzerland, or to the data protection authority of your country of residence in the EEA, such as the CNIL in France.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date on this page shows when it last changed.
