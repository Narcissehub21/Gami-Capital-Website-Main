Gami is made up of two companies. Gami Capital is an investment firm and Gami Labs is a DeFi vault curator. They are separate legal entities that work closely together, and this note explains why we organised them that way.

## What each company does

Gami Capital invests in the companies building onchain infrastructure and onchain finance, such as oracles, vault infrastructure, credit, interest-rate markets, privacy and AI. We take equity and token positions from pre-seed to strategic rounds, and we structure access for qualified investors, family offices and financial institutions.

Gami Labs curates public and private vaults across a restricted set of vetted DeFi protocols, currently Morpho, Aave, Curve DAO, Pendle, Spectra, Stake DAO and Silo Finance. The vaults hold BTC, ETH, USDC and XRP and are rebalanced dynamically, with real-time monitoring and MPC custody. Labs also runs liquidity bootstrapping programmes for emerging DeFi protocols.

## Why they are separate

The two activities serve different clients. A venture investor backs a company for several years and earns a return as it grows. A vault curator manages capital that can be withdrawn at any time and is assessed every month on risk-adjusted yield. Running both in one entity would make it unclear who the client is and whose interest comes first. Separate entities keep mandates, reporting and governance distinct.

## Why they work together

Each company helps the other do its job. Gami Labs deploys capital into live protocols and sees how they behave under stress and which infrastructure holds up. Gami Capital uses that experience when it underwrites new investments.

Gami Capital, for its part, gives Labs access to a network of founders, protocol teams and co-investors, and an early look at infrastructure that will matter in a few years.

Founders can work with both. Gami Capital provides investment, network and structuring, and once the protocol is live, Gami Labs can provide onchain liquidity and vault curation.

## An example

Gami Capital backed [Lagoon](/portfolio/lagoon) at pre-seed in 2024. Lagoon is also one of the vault infrastructure providers Gami Labs builds on. Running live strategies on the product after the investment gave both companies a much deeper understanding of it than due diligence alone would have.

This is why Gami is organised as two companies that work together.
