We expect blockchain infrastructure to become standard across institutional financial services within the next decade. Gami Capital invests in founders who rebuild core financial primitives on these rails, such as payments, credit, settlement, clearing, asset issuance and tokenisation.

Our focus is institutional and enterprise finance, a market of more than $500T in assets where systems are slow, costly and opaque. Retail speculation is outside our scope.

We also think blockchain will change financial services more than AI. Blockchain replaces core systems and workflows. AI mostly improves analysis and decisions on top of systems that stay the same.

## Why the infrastructure has barely changed

Settlement in most markets still runs at T+2, transactions are expensive and access is limited. Much of the technology used to issue money and move it is more than 30 years old.

Fintech had a large impact on consumer finance, where startups competed with banks and venture capital paid for new front ends, open banking and automated decisions. Institutional finance saw far less of that pressure, for three reasons.

- **A knowledge gap.** Most startups and investors lack the technical knowledge of institutional markets needed to change them.
- **Information asymmetry.** A small number of players hold a large share of each market, and asymmetry is part of how they make money. Without understanding the full chain of incentives, and without senior contacts inside institutions, new technology companies struggle to sell.
- **Coordination.** A new platform only works when every party to a transaction uses it. Smaller institutions do not carry enough weight with the sell side or with large issuers to drive adoption from the bottom up.

## Why institutions will acquire this infrastructure

Every large financial institution now sees itself as a technology company, and some have pushed hard, such as JPMorgan with its Onyx platform, now called Kinexys. Even the early movers struggle. Senior management usually comes from the front office, engineering is run as a cost centre, and pay and promotion rarely reward long-term infrastructure work.

Specialised companies fill that gap. We expect the strongest companies being built today to be acquired by financial incumbents within five to seven years, and we aim to invest before that happens.

Institutions are already using these rails. BlackRock's BUIDL fund is accepted as off-exchange collateral at major trading venues. Franklin Templeton runs money market funds onchain. JPMorgan's tokenised collateral network processes repo trades with large buy-side firms, and Goldman Sachs runs tokenised repo transactions. In the United States, the GENIUS Act, signed in July 2025, set a federal framework for payment stablecoins with reserve and disclosure rules. Circle's listing in June 2025 took its valuation from about $7B at the IPO to around $60B at its peak.

## Where the value sits

### Programmable money

Stablecoins and tokenised deposits make it possible to sweep cash continuously instead of daily or monthly, to monitor margin calls automatically and to release payments when conditions are met. In foreign exchange and cross-border payments, early tests by JPMorgan, Deutsche Bank, Standard Chartered and the BIS suggest costs up to ten times lower and settlement moving from T+2 to the same day.

### Clearing and settlement

US private credit issuance is about $1T a year. A typical deal passes through around twelve software systems, many of them more than 20 years old. Settlement takes about a month on average at a cost close to 100bps, and a quarter of trades take six months or more at over 200bps. Smart contracts, shared ledgers and zero-knowledge proofs can cut these operating costs by 30% to 80%.

In our portfolio, [Pareto](/portfolio/pareto) and [Tenka](/portfolio/tenka) work on onchain private credit, and [SuperNova](/portfolio/supernova) on onchain interest-rate markets.

### Portfolio management at scale

When portfolios are written as smart contracts and assets and cash are held as tokens, steps spread across separate systems become one automated process. Programmatic settlement can lower portfolio expenses by about 20% through less idle cash alone.

### Fund share issuance

Issuing and transferring fund shares as tokens on regulated digital asset exchanges can reduce operating costs for investment managers by 10% to 50%. It also gives them more control over distribution and access to a wider client base. Large fund administrators now treat it as a priority.

## Why we favour equity

According to Memento Research, which tracked 118 token launches, 85% of the tokens launched in 2025 trade below their launch price. The median decline is 71% in fully diluted value and 67% in market cap. High launch valuations, aggressive unlock schedules and thin order books all weigh on prices, while venture fundraising has fallen to 12% of its level in the second quarter of 2022, according to Galaxy Research.

We therefore invest mainly through equity, with a SAFE or a SAFE combined with a token warrant. This aligns us more closely with founders and keeps the legal structure simple. We accept SAFTs for protocols whose token model is already proven.

## What it takes to win

The technical moat in this market is moderate. Companies win on go-to-market, which depends on understanding how incentives work inside institutions, on a regulatory strategy that supports it, and on execution to institutional standards.

The best teams pair senior operators from trading, structuring or buy-side desks with engineers who know both Web2 and blockchain. Such teams are rare, and finding them takes networks across blockchain and traditional finance, in several regions.
